## Key Findings (Preliminary)

### Cost Asymmetry Problem
- Patriot interceptor: $3-4M
- Shahed drone: $30K
- **Exchange ratio: 133:1** (prohibitively expensive)

### Recommended Approach
1. **Layered defense** - Multiple systems at different ranges
2. **Right tool for the threat** - Don't use $4M interceptor on $30K drone
3. **Civilian shelters** - Most cost-effective protection
4. **Early warning** - 30 seconds saves lives
