## ✅ 5. Shahed-136 / Geran-2

**Source:** Wikipedia *HESA Shahed 136* (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HESA_Shahed_136`), retrieved 2026-08-29 (aggregates CSIS/Reuters/leaked-doc reporting — see its footnotes)

| Attribute | Repo claim | Verified value from source | Verdict |
|-----------|-----------|---------------------------|---------|
| Speed | ~185 km/h | Shahed class ~185 km/h (comparable FLM-136: 194 km/h max) | ✅ CONFIRMED |
| Range | 1,000–2,000 km | **970–1,500 km** (some estimates to **2,000–2,500 km**) | ⚠️ REFINED |
| Mass | — | 200 kg (up to ~240 kg hardened variants) | ➕ ADDED |
| Cost | ~$30K | **Major evolution — see below** | ❌ **CORRECTED** |
| Guidance | — | GNSS + INS (+ teleoperation on later variants) | ➕ ADDED |

**Cost — most important correction in this audit:**
- Initial 2022 estimates: **$20,000–50,000/unit**
- Leaked Iranian procurement documents (2022): Iran sold 6,000 to Russia at **~$193,000/unit**; ~$290K for a 2,000-unit order
- Leaked docs: Russia projected domestic cost dropping to **~$48,000–48,800/unit**
- April 2024 (RUSI-style analysis *"Geran-2: from low-cost missile to lurking ammunition"*): upgraded/hardened production cost **~$80,000/unit**

⚠️ **Implication for the repo's headline "133:1 cost asymmetry":** using verified procurement costs, the asymmetry is ~20:1 (if $193K) to ~50-80:1 (if $48–80K domestic), **not 133:1**. The 133:1 figure appears based on the initial $30K estimate and should be revised.
